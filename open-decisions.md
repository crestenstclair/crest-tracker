# Crest Tracker — open decisions and capability gates

Updated 15 October 2026. These are not missing user requirements; they are the remaining choices needed to turn those requirements into reliable code. Use small repository investigations and focused design reviews to resolve them. Do not reopen confirmed boundaries without a reason.

The [full implementation plan](implementation-plan.md) assigns M0–M10 gates. Full Synth functionality/rendering reuse, a hard synth/sequencer layer and dependency-based co-development are now confirmed. Section 3 defines local source overrides, paired edit/test/commit work and pinned clean builds; native Synth pages keep their own reducer/rendering path. Section 5's musical defaults and M6's resource policy remain proposals unless accepted separately. Existing Phase references below are conceptual; the plan defines execution order.

## Confirmed choices not to revisit accidentally

One Song is the sole document; New/Open replaces it and Save/Save As preserves it. No parent container or child-song management. Shared sounds keep saved revisions until explicit update; routing/mix is song-local. Also confirmed: Piano Roll phrase type with external MIDI/MPE recording as its primary workflow, explicit instrument/channel routing, gamepad editing, nudging and grid quantize; Synth separation; controller-first semantic input; Song→Track→Chain→Phrase hierarchy; 16 tracks; per-note instrument/channel target; shared definitions with independent occurrence cursors; one NoteData representation and musical timeline; subdivisions through 1/64; phrase MIDI FX from the start; three FX slots; no tables; decimal presentation; scale-aware note entry with explicit escape paths; literal chord quality; read-only Default and editable Custom chord banks; contextual popovers; main-menu Mixer/Sends/Settings; Expanded and Minimap as views of the same composition.
